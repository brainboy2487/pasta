1. State‑Aware Health Monitors (The “Nervous System”)

These are lightweight processes that constantly check:

    Service uptime

    Latency and throughput

    Memory/CPU anomalies

    Model response entropy (detects when outputs become unstable)

    File integrity and config drift

    Queue backlogs or event‑bus stalls

Why it works:
It gives the AI a real‑time picture of its own health. When something deviates from the baseline, the system knows before failure occurs.

Best tools:

    systemd health checks

    Watchdogs

    Heartbeat daemons

    Hash‑based integrity checks
